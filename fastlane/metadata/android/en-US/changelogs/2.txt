Offline Checker 1.0

• Lists visible apps and shows whether each has the INTERNET permission.
• Apps with INTERNET show received and sent data for the last 7 days or 24 hours (7 days by default).
• Apps without INTERNET are marked "No INTERNET permission".
• Sort by usage (default) or app name from the sort badge.
• App icons in the list, and a system-apps toggle in the top bar (off by default).
• No INTERNET permission: all checks are read on-device and never leave it.
